## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated: static HTML5, Tailwind CSS CDN, and vanilla JavaScript as explicitly required by the brief.

## Users
Recruiters, professional contacts, and visitors who want to understand Mexi Milianus's work experience, management education, customer service skills, and interest in entrepreneurship.

## Product Purpose
A public personal portfolio for the meximlians identity. Success means visitors can quickly understand Mexi's professional direction and reach him through the approved contact channels.

## Positioning
A personal story connecting real F&B service experience, management studies, and hands-on exploration of a student woodcraft business.

## Operating Context
A single-page static website in Bahasa Indonesia, intended for direct local opening or deployment to static hosting.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Anchor navigation, mobile menu, contact links, and a three-image documentation gallery. Do not publish the CV, add unprovided personal data, invent dates or metrics, or imply that the F&B business concept is operating. Domain and hosting are not needed according to the latest user direction. No paid external service, DNS changes, or accounts are authorized.

## Brand Commitments
Public identity: meximlians. Personal headline name: Mexi Milianus. Primary tagline: “Kopi Nikmat, Teman Setiap Cerita.” Voice: natural, professional, and personal. Palette and editorial portfolio direction are defined in the user brief.

## Evidence on Hand
The user's detailed portfolio brief is available. The supplied CV is present in the project root; its embedded profile image was extracted for the hero. The Google Drive profile photo link could not be accessed with the available web tool. No achievement documentation photos are available; placeholders must remain explicit and must not imply authentic documentation.

## Product Principles
- Keep every public claim traceable to the supplied brief or an accessible source.
- Present the work as a personal portfolio, not a coffee shop or company site.
- Connect service work and business interests without overstating either.
- Keep contact details limited to the approved public channels.
